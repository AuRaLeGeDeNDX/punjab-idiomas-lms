Video URL Diagnostic

Instructions:

  1. Open your browser console (F12)
  2. Go to the page with the video
  3. Run this in console: document.querySelector('video source').src
  4. Copy the full URL and paste it here

What to check:

  • Does the URL have signature= and expires= parameters?
  • Is the content ID a simple number (like /secure/video/3)?
  • Or is it malformed (like /secure/video/3..6a3bba5aca3cb899e:1)?

Quick Fix Test:

Try accessing this URL directly in your browser (replace 3 with your actual content ID):

http://localhost:8000/secure/video/3?signature=test&expires=9999999999

This should give you a more specific error message about what's wrong.

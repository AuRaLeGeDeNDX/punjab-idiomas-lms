## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Go to the LMS website
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click "Log In"

**Forgot Password?**
- Click "Forgot Password" on login page
- Enter your email
- Follow reset instructions

### Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your dashboard with:

- **Upcoming Assignments**: Due within 7 days
- **Overdue Assignments**: Past due, still accepting submissions
- **Missed Assignments**: Past due, no longer accepting submissions
- **Recent Grades**: Your latest graded work
- **Performance Chart**: Your score trends over time

### Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:
- New assignments published
- Upcoming due dates (24 hours before)
- Grades published
- Teacher feedback

**Managing Notifications**:
1. Click your profile icon
2. Select "Notification Preferences"
3. Toggle email and in-app notifications
4. Save changes

## Submitting Work

### Text Submissions

For text-only or text+file assignments:

1. Open the assignment
2. Click **"Start Submission"** or **"Edit Submission"**
3. Type or paste your text in the editor
4. Format your text if needed (bold, italic, lists)
5. Click **"Save Draft"** to save without submitting
6. Click **"Submit"** when ready

**Text Editor Features**:
- Bold, italic, underline
- Bullet and numbered lists
- Links
- Copy/paste from Word (formatting preserved)

### File Submissions

For file-only or text+file assignments:

1. Open the assignment
2. Click **"Start Submission"** or **"Edit Submission"**
3. Click **"Choose Files"** or drag files to upload area
4. Select file(s) from your computer
5. Wait for upload to complete (progress bar shows status)
6. Click **"Submit"** when ready

**Accepted File Types**:
- Documents: PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT
- Images: JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF
- Archives: ZIP, RAR

**File Size Limits**:
- Per file: 10 MB maximum
- Total submission: 50 MB maximum

**Tips for File Uploads**:
- Use PDF for final submissions (preserves formatting)
- Name files clearly (e.g., "Smith_Essay_Final.pdf")
- Compress large files before uploading
- Check file uploaded successfully before submitting

### Saving Drafts

You can save your work without submitting:

1. Enter your text or upload files
2. Click **"Save Draft"**
3. Come back later to continue
4. Click **"Submit"** when finished

**Benefits**:
- Work in multiple sessions
- Don't lose your progress
- Review before final submission
- No pressure to finish in one sitting

**Note**: Drafts are NOT submissions. You must click "Submit" to turn in your work!

### Submitting Your Work

When you're ready to submit:

1. Review your text and files
2. Check you've met all requirements
3. Click **"Submit Assignment"**
4. Confirm submission in popup
5. See confirmation message

**After Submitting**:
- You'll see "Submitted" status
- Submission timestamp recorded
- Teacher receives notification
- You can still edit if allowed

### Editing Submissions

If your teacher allows editing before grading:

1. Open the assignment
2. Click **"Edit Submission"**
3. Make your changes
4. Click **"Submit"** again

**Important**:
- Each edit creates a new version
- Teacher can see all versions
- Can't edit after teacher starts grading
- Can't edit after due date (unless late submissions allowed)

### Late Submissions

If late submissions are allowed:

**Before Due Date**:
- Submit normally
- No "Late" marking

**After Due Date**:
- You can still submit
- Marked as "Late"
- Teacher decides if/how to penalize

**If Late Submissions NOT Allowed**:
- Can't submit after due date
- Assignment marked as "Missed"
- Contact teacher if special circumstances

## Tracking Your Progress

### Your Progress Dashboard

Access from: **Dashboard → My Progress**

**What You'll See**:
- **Completion Progress**: Percentage of assignments completed
- **Average Score**: Your overall grade average
- **Upcoming Assignments**: Due soon (with countdown)
- **Overdue Assignments**: Past due but still accepting submissions
- **Missed Assignments**: Past due, no longer accepting
- **Recent Grades**: Last 5 graded assignments
- **Performance Chart**: Your scores over time

### Understanding Your Stats

**Completion Progress**:
- Shows % of assignments you've submitted
- Goal: Keep this above 90%
- Missing assignments lower this number

**Average Score**:
- Mean of all your graded assignments
- Updates when new grades published
- Reflects your overall performance

**Performance Trend**:
- Line chart showing scores over time
- Going up = improving
- Going down = may need help
- Flat = consistent performance

### Assignment History

View all your assignments:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Assignment History**
2. See complete list with:
   - Assignment title
   - Due date
   - Status
   - Score (if graded)
   - Submission date

**Filter Options**:
- By course
- By status (submitted, graded, etc.)
- By date range

### Upcoming Deadlines

Stay on top of due dates:

**Dashboard Widget**:
- Shows next 7 days
- Color-coded by urgency:
  - 🔴 Due today
  - 🟠 Due tomorrow
  - 🟡 Due this week

**Calendar View**:
1. Click **"Calendar"** in navigation
2. See all assignments on calendar
3. Click date to see details

**Email Reminders**:
- Sent 24 hours before due date
- Only if you haven't submitted
- Can disable in notification preferences

## Understanding Grades

### Viewing Your Grades

**From Dashboard**:
- See recent grades in "Recent Grades" widget
- Click to view details

**From Assignment**:
- Open the assignment
- See grade at the top if published
- Click "View Grade Details"

### Grade Information

When viewing a grade, you'll see:

**Score**:
- Your points earned
- Maximum points possible
- Percentage

**Feedback**:
- Teacher's comments
- Suggestions for improvement
- Praise for good work

**Rubric Scores** (if used):
- Score for each criterion
- What you did well
- Where to improve

**Submission Details**:
- When you submitted
- If it was late
- Files you uploaded

### Grade Status

**Draft**:
- Teacher is still grading
- Not visible to you yet
- Be patient!

**Published**:
- Grade is final and visible
- You receive notification
- Can view anytime

**Locked**:
- Grade is finalized
- Can't be changed (except by admin)
- Usually for official reporting

### Understanding Rubrics

If your assignment has a rubric:

**What It Shows**:
- Different criteria (categories)
- Points possible for each
- Points you earned for each
- Total score

**Example**:
```
Essay Rubric (100 points)

Content Quality: 35/40
- Good analysis and evidence
- Could use more depth

Organization: 28/30
- Clear structure
- Excellent transitions

Grammar: 27/30
- Minor spelling errors
- Good overall mechanics

Total: 90/100 (90%)
```

**How to Use It**:
- See exactly where you excelled
- Identify areas to improve
- Understand teacher's expectations
- Guide future submissions

### Requesting Grade Review

If you believe there's an error:

1. Review the rubric and feedback carefully
2. Check your submission matches what was graded
3. Email your teacher politely:
   - Explain your concern
   - Provide specific examples
   - Ask for clarification
4. Wait for teacher response

**Tips**:
- Be respectful and professional
- Focus on understanding, not arguing
- Provide evidence for your case
- Accept the final decision gracefully

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### "Can't see assignment"

**Possible Causes**:
- Assignment not published yet
- Not enrolled in course
- Browser cache issue

**Solutions**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Check you're in correct course
3. Clear browser cache
4. Contact teacher if still missing

#### "File won't upload"

**Possible Causes**:
- File too large (>10MB)
- Wrong file type
- Internet connection issue
- Browser problem

**Solutions**:
1. Check file size (compress if needed)
2. Verify file type is accepted
3. Check internet connection
4. Try different browser
5. Contact IT support

#### "Lost my work"

**Possible Causes**:
- Didn't save draft
- Browser crashed
- Session timed out

**Solutions**:
1. Check if draft was auto-saved
2. Look in browser history
3. Check Downloads folder
4. Contact teacher to explain situation

**Prevention**:
- Save drafts frequently
- Work in a document editor first
- Copy text before submitting
- Keep backup copies

#### "Submitted wrong file"

**Solutions**:
1. If before due date: Edit submission and upload correct file
2. If after due date: Email teacher immediately
3. Explain the mistake
4. Attach correct file to email
5. Ask if you can resubmit

#### "Can't edit submission"

**Possible Causes**:
- Teacher started grading
- Past due date
- Editing not allowed

**Solutions**:
1. Check assignment settings
2. Email teacher to request edit permission
3. Explain what needs changing
4. Submit correction via email if needed

### Technical Problems

**Browser Issues**:
- Use Chrome, Firefox, or Safari (latest version)
- Clear cache and cookies
- Disable browser extensions
- Try incognito/private mode

**Upload Problems**:
- Check file size and type
- Try smaller files
- Use wired internet if possible
- Upload one file at a time

**Display Issues**:
- Zoom level at 100%
- Screen resolution at least 1024x768
- JavaScript enabled
- Pop-up blocker disabled for LMS

## FAQs

### Submissions

**Q: Can I submit early?**  
A: Yes! Submit anytime before the due date.

**Q: Can I edit after submitting?**  
A: Usually yes, until your teacher starts grading or the due date passes.

**Q: What if I submit the wrong file?**  
A: Edit your submission immediately if possible, or email your teacher.

**Q: How do I know my submission went through?**  
A: You'll see a confirmation message and "Submitted" status.

**Q: Can I submit after the due date?**  
A: Only if the assignment allows late submissions. It will be marked "Late."

**Q: How many times can I edit?**  
A: As many times as needed before grading starts, but each edit is tracked.

### Files

**Q: What file types can I upload?**  
A: PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, ZIP, RAR

**Q: What's the file size limit?**  
A: 10MB per file, 50MB total per submission

**Q: Can I upload multiple files?**  
A: Yes, up to 5 files per submission (if assignment allows)

**Q: Should I use PDF or Word?**  
A: PDF is better for final submissions (preserves formatting)

**Q: Can I upload from my phone?**  
A: Yes, but desktop is recommended for better experience

### Grades

**Q: When will I get my grade?**  
A: Typically within 1 week of submission, but varies by teacher

**Q: Why can't I see my grade?**  
A: Teacher may still be grading or hasn't published it yet

**Q: Can I dispute a grade?**  
A: Yes, email your teacher respectfully with specific concerns

**Q: What does "Draft" grade mean?**  
A: Teacher is still grading; you can't see it yet

**Q: Will late submissions be penalized?**  
A: Depends on teacher's policy; check assignment details

### Technical

**Q: What browser should I use?**  
A: Chrome, Firefox, or Safari (latest versions)

**Q: Can I use my phone?**  
A: Yes, but desktop is recommended for submissions

**Q: What if the site is down?**  
A: Check status page or contact IT support

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page

**Q: Can I access from off-campus?**  
A: Yes, from anywhere with internet
